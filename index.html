research

01 · physical AI

AI that perceives, understands, and acts in the physical world. I am interested in giving robots physical common sense (contact, deformation, and dynamics) so that what they perceive and learn turns into safe, reliable actions on real hardware, from soft and continuum robots to everyday manipulation.

embodied intelligence · world models · vision-language-action · sim-to-real

02 · robot imagination

Letting robots mentally simulate interactions with objects they have never seen before acting. By combining physics-based simulation, geometric reasoning, and learning from demonstrations and large language models, a robot can imagine what an unfamiliar object affords and how to use it, without massive labeled datasets. See the lab's affordance imagination project.

affordance imagination · physics simulation · LLM reasoning · unseen objects

03 · continuum robots

Lightweight kinematic, static, and dynamic models for cable-, tendon-, and rod-driven continuum robots built on actuation-space energy formulations, together with data-efficient learning from demonstrations for whole-body soft grasping and compliant continuum joints with tunable stiffness.

actuation-space modeling · flow matching · compliant mechanisms
