03 · continuum robots

Lightweight kinematic, static, and dynamic models for cable-, tendon-, and rod-driven continuum robots built on actuation-space energy formulations, together with data-efficient learning from demonstrations for whole-body soft grasping and compliant continuum joints with tunable stiffness.

actuation-space modeling · flow matching · compliant mechanisms

projects

01 · flow-matching soft grasping

An actuation-space learning framework that uses flow matching for whole-body soft robotic grasping. It reaches a 97.5% success rate and generalizes well from only 30 demonstrations. I led the algorithm implementation and experimental validation.

MBZUAI · 2025.03–2026.03 · RA-L 2026

02 · compliant continuum joints

Studied how structural parameters of a spatial curved-beam continuum joint shape its flexibility and stiffness, and proposed an adjustable-stiffness design approach.

Chongqing University · 2023.09–2026.06 · master's thesis

03 · slope nandina planting machine

An integrated transport-and-planting machine for Nandina domestica on mountain slopes, with adaptive leveling, linkage feeding, and positioning drill/backfill modules. It won the National First Prize and became a granted invention patent.

project leader · 2021.10–2022.08 · ecological restoration
